## 🔄 HOW IT WORKS

### **Step 1: Goal Ingest & Deconstruction**
Type a simple business target (e.g., *"Deploy an automated cryptographic trust locker system"*). Our parent Orchestrator instantly parses the prompt, evaluates complexity, and commissions a custom 4-agent fleet.

### **Step 2: Sequential Pipeline Assembly**
Agents run in a deterministic sequence, feeding completed findings downstream. Research uncovers market gaps; Product structures user flows; Strategy outlines monetize hooks; Critic audits cross-border legal barriers.

### **Step 3: Consensus Scoring & Assembly**
The Orchestrator aggregates all independent reviews, generates a mathematical consensus score (e.g., 94%), and outputs print-ready 7-day milestone checklists, compliance warnings, and monetization matrices.

### **Step 4: Continuous Strategic Assistance**
Engage with the Founder Copilot, utilizing the synthesized multi-agent memory to draft compliance forms, outline database schemas, or plan your marketing campaign.
